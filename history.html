Early Life

Burna Boy was born Damini Ebunoluwa Ogulu on July 2, 1991, in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. Music ran in his family: his grandfather, Benson Idonije, was a respected music critic who once managed the Afrobeat pioneer Fela Kuti. Growing up, Burna Boy absorbed Fela's music alongside dancehall, reggae, and hip-hop, and he began producing music as a teenager. He later spent time studying in the United Kingdom before returning to Nigeria to pursue music full time.

Breakthrough

His 2012 single Like to Party made him a household name in Nigeria, and his debut album L.I.F.E. followed in 2013. He continued to build his audience with On a Spaceship (2015) and then signed internationally, releasing Outside in 2018 with hits like Ye.

The African Giant

His 2019 album African Giant earned him his first Grammy nomination. In 2021 his album Twice as Tall won the Grammy Award for Best Global Music Album, making him one of the most celebrated African artists of his generation.

Global Superstar

Love, Damini (2022) produced the worldwide hit Last Last. In 2023 he made history by headlining stadium shows, including London Stadium and New York's Citi Field, among the first African artists to sell out stadiums in the UK and the US. He followed with I Told Them... (2023) and continues to carry Afro-fusion to audiences around the world.

Timeline
